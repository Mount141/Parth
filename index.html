The problem

Falls are the leading cause of injury among older adults — and every minute of delayed response increases the risk of serious harm.

1 in 4
seniors aged 65+ fall each year
~20 min
average time before a fall is noticed without monitoring
1 in 5
falls cause a serious injury requiring medical care

Our solution

AI Vision Care watches for falls in real time and alerts staff immediately — without recording or storing video.

🔒
Privacy-preserving
Only skeletal pose points are analyzed on-device. No video is stored or transmitted.
⚡
Real-time detection
Falls are identified within seconds using on-site AI processing — no cloud round-trip.
📲
Multi-channel alerts
Staff dashboard and Telegram notifications fire the moment a fall is confirmed.
