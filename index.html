Meet Our Director

Parth – Director of Tiny Tot Early Years

Hi, my name is Parth. I have been in the Early Childhood field for 35 years and employed with Tiny Tot Early Years Centre for the past 26 years. I have a diploma in Early Childhood Education from Holland College. I have attended numerous workshops and conferences to stay updated with new learning strategies. I am familiar with all age groups, but have spent the majority of my teaching within Pre-Kindergarten and School-Age programs. I am currently enrolled in UNB’s Bachelor Of Education in Early Childhood Degree Program. I hosted a school-age workshop titled “Too Cool for Daycare” in November 2011 for educators involved in after school care. I currently live in the Charlottetown area and have two children and two grandchildren.
